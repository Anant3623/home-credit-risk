    Before you scale this

    Reject inference. Every applicant in this data already received a loan. The simulator re-ranks borrowers the lender approved in the past; it cannot say how people the lender rejected would have repaid. A real expansion would need a controlled test (for example, approving a small random slice above the cut-off) before scaling.

    This page uses monthly credit-bureau history (loans at other lenders, up to 96 months before application). Region rating, contract and risk band filters apply. The data split and profile filters such as income or education do not, because the history tables were pre-aggregated by band, contract and region.

    Limits to state in any interview

    • Reject inference: outcomes exist only for applicants who got a loan, so approval scenarios re-rank past borrowers rather than predict rejected applicants.
    • TARGET is a proxy: it flags repayment difficulty, not a measured accounting default or loss.
    • Expected loss is a scenario: PD × assumed LGD × credit amount. There are no recovery data to estimate LGD.
    • No calendar dates: history is relative to each application, so the model is validated on a random split, not on a later time period.
    • Gender is one of the 25 model features. Most lenders exclude protected attributes from credit decisions; remove it and re-check AUC before presenting the model as decision-ready.
    • Not Indian data: Home Credit's international book, anonymised, used here for non-commercial portfolio work.

    Repayment difficulty (TARGET = 1) means the client had late payments beyond a threshold on at least one of the first instalments. Rates use all applicants in the selection; Wilson 95% intervals where shown. Amounts are in the dataset's currency units.

    Data: Home Credit Default Risk (Kaggle), anonymised; raw files are not redistributed.
